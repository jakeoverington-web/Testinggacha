| 20 | Art direction | WLOP is the art guideline: dark painterly fantasy; adult/NSFW content | Replaces the earlier Omni Heroes / Azur Lane direction. Inspired, never copied (no redraws of his characters or compositions). Every character is an adult and drawn as one, with adult faces and proportions. Mythic Heroes is a reference for systems only. Full study: WLOP Style Study (Claude Docs) | 2026-10-02 |

| 26 | Combat rules v1 | Shared stat sheet, effect library and control rules | Energy 100 (basic hit 10, 0.5 per 1% HP lost, kill 20); Haste divides cooldowns; effect resist floor 15%; two-tier effects; shields stack to 50% max HP; hard control max 2.5s with diminishing returns; Ocean race keyword Soaked. Lives in combat.json; all numbers are tuning starting points | 2026-10-02 |
| 27 | World lore | Central conflict: the Starfall War | Every elf lives while her star burns; the stars are dying (the Dimming) and the starless turn Hollow. One fallen star (the Lamp) can rekindle enough light for one race, so all five fight for it. No race is the villain; each has an old crime that explains its counter (row 3). Player = the Lampbearer, a starless elf the Lamp cannot burn; heroes of any race bind to her shard (the Wick), which explains mixed teams. Hollow = common campaign enemy. Tone: grief, guilt and cost, not gore; adults only; sensual art kept apart from harm. Race names: Lumarin (High), Noctyr (Dark), Verdani (Nature), Thalyri (Ocean), Aethari (Arcane); world Ilvareth. Race ids stay high/dark/nature/ocean/arcane. Full lore incl. 60 hero bios: World Bible (Claude Docs) | 2026-10-02 |
| 28 | Battle rules v1 | Built and tested in Core: 0.1 s ticks, 90 s limit (timeout = attacker loses), all 60 kits as data | Hard control: one at a time by priority (Stasis > Charm > Fear > Stun > Airborne > Sleep > Root), max 2.5 s, 2nd within 4 s halved, then resisted until the 6 s window ends. 15% resist floor on every enemy debuff. Auto attacks deal 75% ATK by default, 100% for auto-attack specialists and 60% for casters (heroes.json basicPct, set in tools/roster/kits.py), and casting pauses attacks for 0.1 s, so skills carry more of each kit (ablation report). All heroes get 1.2x HP in battle (battle.json hpScale): fights about 31 s, 5.7% timeouts. AI aim: sleep and stasis go on an enemy no ally is hitting; plant-yourself skills wait for an enemy in reach. Marked enemies preferred by every hero. Control and stealth payoffs also count for 3 s after the effect ends (conditions like stun+3), because control lasts 1-2.5 s and sleep breaks on the first hit (approved 2026-10-03, pass 9). Drained: an enemy who loses energy to an ability is Drained for 4 s (pass 10). Abilities wait until they have a worthwhile target. Gaps in kit text filled with defaults listed in docs/design/battle-rules.md (interpretations awaiting owner review). First balance sweep: docs/design/balance-sweep-v1.md | 2026-10-02 |
| 29 | Balance target | Team strength comes from synergies and buffs; many different teams are top-tier; single heroes stay modest | Owner's direction (2026-10-03). Heroes: mild situational play (race bonus stays +10%), each hero's overall uplift within about ±8 points of a random same-role replacement. Teams: building around a synergy package (shared keywords such as Soaked, Burn, Mark, Energy) should clearly beat a random balanced team, and the strongest teams should use many different packages, races and heroes. Measured by the synergy report plus the niche, ablation and interaction reports in docs/design/. Roster pass 9 (approved 2026-10-03): payoffs +35-60%, one team-wide amplifier per keyword, stronger DEF-down (50%) and weaken (40%) tier, carry trims, weak-hero lifts; result: 1 hero outside ±8 (was 14), top 10 teams use 10-11 packages, best packages +14, 19 of 27 keywords still lost to a random team. Pass 10 (row 30): all 60 heroes within ±8; every top-10 team runs a synergy (synergy-report-v3, package-probe-v2, niche-report-v8, interaction-audit-v3) | 2026-10-03 |
| 30 | Synergy packages | 12 packages, each naming ONE kind of condition, on the enemy or on your own team, never both | Owner's rule (2026-10-03). Enemy: Burn; Soaked; Wounds (bleeding or poisoned); Hindered (slowed, rooted, stunned or knocked up); Dread (asleep, feared or charmed); Exposed (marked, weakened, DEF-lowered or cursed); Disrupted (blinded, silenced or Drained); Gathered (pulled together or taunted). Team: Lifesteal; Stealth (hidden, or within 3 s of leaving it); Tempo (energy and faster cooldowns); Guarded (shields or buffs). Kill, Dispel, Isolated and Summon are hero traits, not packages. Each package has payers and at least one team-wide amplifier; no two heroes share a passive. Replaces 27 single keywords (roster pass 10) | 2026-10-03 |
| 31 | Levels and stars | Max level 200; 10 star ranks: 1-5 gold, 6-10 diamond, diamond border at 10 | Top five level with Hero XP and gold, capped by stars (1-10: 60, 70, 80, 90, 100, 120, 140, 160, 180, 200); everyone else syncs to the 5th-highest whatever their stars (row 7). Max stars costs 8 copies + 15 race fodder + 4.1M gold: gold stars mostly copies, diamond stars mostly fodder + gold; per-step numbers tuned by the phase 2 economy sim. Free full refund of levels and stars any time. heroes.json still caps stars at 5 and changes in phase 2. Plan: Game Modes and Progression Plan (Claude Docs), decisions 1-3, 12 | 2026-10-03 |
| 32 | Fodder | A spare 1-star hero of the same race, or a Race Sigil of that race | Starred heroes are reset (refunded) before feeding. Sigils from race towers, homeland chapter bosses and idle loot from ch 5 (plan decision 4) | 2026-10-03 |
| 33 | Idle loot | 24 h hard cap | Rate set by highest cleared stage; nothing accrues after 24 h until you collect. No paid fast-forward; Hourglasses (2 h of loot) from tower and boss milestones (plan decision 5) | 2026-10-03 |
| 34 | Campaign and pacing | 20 chapters x 30 stages = 600 (ch01_s01 to ch20_s30), more added as data | About 700 h of idle income (a month of daily collection) takes the top five to level 200. Every 10th stage a gate, stage 30 the chapter boss; enemies are Hollow echoes of the 60 heroes (plan decisions 6, 13). Generated by StageGen: chapter 1 fields 3 enemies and chapter 2 fields 4, without synergy preference on normal stages (owner, 2026-10-03); each stage's enemy level is the lowest at which the best of 4 reference teams wins 50-70% (40-55% in a chapter's last 5 stages), modelling a player who swaps team after seeing the enemy (row 18; owner, 2026-10-03). Gate: docs/design/campaign-gate-v1.md | 2026-10-03 |

| 36 | Rivals (PvP) | Ladder of 100 fixed bot teams; climb by beating the team above | Each rival is a named captain with a synergy team; bots fight at your synced level and stars, +0% at rank 100 to +25% at rank 1. | 37 | Boss mode | 5 bosses, always open, 90 s damage fights, endless difficulty, no attempt limit | 10 damage thresholds per difficulty, about 1.6x apart; rewards only the first time each is passed. Built as the reusable special encounter (row 11) (plan decision 10) | 2026-10-03 |
| 38 | Towers | Endless Stair (any heroes) + 5 race towers (one race only); endless, no daily limit | Floors are data; every 10th race-tower floor is guarded by its counter race; race towers drop Race Sigils. Hook: rule floors every 50th (plan decision 11) | 2026-10-03 |
| 39 | Pre-battle and battle screens | Stage panel on the map; pre-battle on the battlefield; live battle HUD | Tap a stage: panel with enemy lineup and Fight. Pre-battle: both teams at their start cells, tap a hero then a slot to swap (2 front / 3 back; 11 formations stay a hook), roster strip with race tabs, Quick Deploy (top 5 by power, tanks in front: the no-strategy option). Power = sqrt(effective HP x damage output) on level/star-scaled stats: EHP = HP x (1 + DEF/defK), damage = ATK x attack speed x crit factor; skills not counted, so damage dealers score highest (owner, 2026-10-03; replaced HP/10 + ATK + DEF, which favoured tanks), Auto-Battle and Battle buttons. Campaign auto-chaining always uses Auto-Battle. Spec: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-03-prebattle-battle-redesign-design.md | 2026-10-03 |
